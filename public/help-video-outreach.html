Overview

Video Outreach finds ecommerce brands that advertise on Meta (Facebook and Instagram), scores them out of 100, picks the product a sample video should be made for, writes the LinkedIn and email messages, and tracks the outreach. You make the video outside SitePounce and paste the link in. It lives under the 🎬 Video Outreach button in the left sidebar.

The whole loop

1. CampaignYou say what to look for: keywords, country, size, caps.
2. RunPulls live ads, adds company and contact data, reads the store, scores, drafts messages. 3 to 10 minutes.
3. ProspectsA list sorted by Priority Number. Open one to see why, what to film, and the messages.
4. ConnectSend the LinkedIn request, by hand with Copy or automatically once a provider is connected.
5. AcceptedThe brand appears on Ready to send with the draft of Message A.
6. SendPaste the video link, check the words, click Send. Never without that click.
7. Follow-upsDrafts at 3 and 7 days. You send them, or switch on auto follow-ups.
8. ReplyStage moves to Replied, follow-ups cancel, you get an email. Then Call booked, Pilot, Client.

What it never does

  • It never sends Message A (the one with the video) on its own. You paste the link and click Send.
  • It never answers a reply. It records it, cancels follow-ups and emails you.
  • It never sends follow-ups unless you switch on auto follow-ups for that campaign.
  • It never contacts a brand twice. A brand belongs to one campaign, and dedupe runs on every pull.

What happens, and when you step in

Where you come in

  1. After a run: open the top brands and fill the four hand-checked signals (DM active, other paid channels, pays for creators, trigger). Two minutes each. Add a contact where the run found none.
  2. When the "new lead" text arrives: make the AI video for the product named in the text.
  3. Ready to send: paste the link, click Send. This is the only send that ever needs you.
  4. Due follow-ups: click Send now on the day, unless auto follow-ups is on.
  5. When a reply text arrives: answer them yourself on LinkedIn, then set the stage on the prospect so Results stay true.

If they do not connect

Nothing is chased on LinkedIn. After 7 days the request shows Pending; after 21 days it is withdrawn and the brand goes Dead, unless you have used email or Instagram with them, in which case it just stays as it is. If they have an email on file you can send Message B by email from the prospect page at any point.

Setting up a campaign, step by step

  1. Video Outreach, Campaigns, New campaign.
  2. Name: what you will recognise later, for example "US Collagen Powder".
  3. Industry: the product area. Click Suggest keywords. Remove research phrases, keep buying phrases.
  4. Country: US. Size band: 1-10 and 11-50. Store platform: Shopify only. Meta advertisers only: ticked.
  5. Target prospects per run 20, raw cap 100, cost cap £10, minimum score 55. These are the defaults.
  6. Schedule: One-off for a first run, then change to Weekly on Monday once you like what it finds.
  7. Service profile: check the name, sender and offer line read the way you want. Message templates: leave blank.
  8. Automation: tick Auto-send connection requests and set the priority cut-off (3 for quality, 4 for volume). Tick Notify me when a run finishes.
  9. Save, then Run now. 3 to 10 minutes. When it finishes, Quick check the top brands, then let it run.

FAQs

Common questions

Will it send anything today?Only weekdays, 8am to 6pm US Eastern (1pm to 11pm UK). Weekends nothing goes out.
How many requests a day?One at a time, a random 10 to 30 minutes apart inside that window, up to the daily cap in Settings (20, hard limit 25) and 100 a week.
I have two campaigns. How are the 20 requests a day shared?They are not split. The caps are one pool across all campaigns. Which brands get a request?Priority 1 to 3 (Must target, Strong, Possible) that have a LinkedIn contact, on campaigns with "Auto-send connection requests" ticked. Change the priority cut-off on the campaign or in Settings.
Why has a good brand not been contacted?No LinkedIn contact on it, or its score is below Possible. Open it, add the contact or fill the hand-checked signals.
Does it ever send the video message on its own?No. Message A only goes when you paste the link and click Send.
Does it answer replies?No. It records them, reads the tone, texts and emails you.
Where do new brands come from each week?Set the campaign Schedule to Weekly (or Daily). Each run only adds brands it has not seen. Daily at raw cap 100 uses about 200 Apollo credits a day, so Weekly is the sensible default.
What does it cost to run?About £3 of Apify and 200 Apollo credits per 100 brands found, plus Unipile's monthly fee for the LinkedIn link.
What if LinkedIn complains?Everything pauses on its own, a red banner appears, you get an email. Check your LinkedIn account, then Resume in Settings.
Can someone else get the alerts?Yes. Settings, Reply alerts by SMS: one line per person, first name then mobile.

Campaigns

Creating one

  1. Campaigns tab, New campaign.
  2. Type the product area in Industry (for example "face creams") and click Suggest keywords. It writes 10 to 20 shopper search terms into the box.
  3. Delete research phrases (review, dosage, vs powder). Keep buying phrases. These keywords drive the whole search, the Industry label does not.
  4. Give it a name, leave the defaults, Save, then Run now.
Hover any ? next to a field for a plain-English explanation and, on prospect fields, the points it earns.

Schedules

  • One-off runs when you click Run now.
  • Daily, Weekly (tick the days) and Monthly (the 1st) run on their own at the run time in the chosen time zone. The worker checks every 10 minutes and starts the run after the run time. Each run only adds brands it has not seen before.
  • End condition: stop after a date, or once the campaign holds N prospects. Blank means until you pause it.
  • Re-check cadence: every 30 days existing prospects get fresh ad counts and a fresh score, so stale brands drift down.

Message templates

Leave the boxes blank to use the standard wording (shown in grey under each box). To change the wording for this campaign only, write your own using the placeholders {first} {service_name} {observation} {product} {video_url} {signoff} {sender_first} {offer_line} {pilot_line}. A blank line (or " / ") separates paragraphs. Every message is checked before it is stored: paragraphs separated, sign-off present, no link in the connection note, "[insert URL here]" in Message A until a video link is pasted.

Automation switches

  • Auto follow-ups: Follow-up 1 and 2 go out on their own 3 and 7 days after Message 1, on the channel Message 1 used. Off means they wait as drafts.
  • Notify me when a run finishes: an email summary.
  • Auto-send connection requests (only once a LinkedIn provider is connected): lets the worker send requests for priority 1 and 2 within the caps in Settings.

Runs

What a run does

  1. Pull ads from the Meta Ad Library for every keyword and country (about 10 ads per brand requested). This runs in the background on Apify and takes 1 to 8 minutes depending on size.
  2. Count each brand: the keyword search only shows a few ads per brand, so each brand's own page is counted (newest 30 active ads). Enough to place every scoring band.
  3. Merge with Apollo company search on domain, remove the exclusion list, household names and brands already in any campaign.
  4. For every brand, in order: hard filters (country, exclusion list, over 200 employees, no active ads, no store), ad analysis (counts, video share, new ads, whether they pay creators, the AI writes the one personal line), store products and the photo rule, contacts (founder or growth lead, enriched once for the full name and LinkedIn link), score, messages.

The line underneath says what it is doing. You can close the page: the worker finishes the run within 10 minutes.

When it stops

Done when the target is reached or every candidate is processed. Stopped (cap) when the raw cap or the cost cap is reached. Failed only on a provider error it cannot recover from, keeping everything processed so far. Stop run stops it by hand.

What it costs

Measured on 20-brand live runs: about £1.60 in total (Apify £0.45, Apollo about 45 credits, AI under £0.20). A 100-brand run is roughly five times that. The estimate on the campaign page is calculated before you run.

Refreshing ad counts

On the campaign page, Refresh ad counts re-pulls each brand's own newest 30 ads and re-scores it. Use it after a run whose per-brand count was skipped (the Runs table says so) or when counts look stale. About 30 Apify rows per brand.

Redoing a run

On the campaign page, the Runs table lists every run with counts, cost and the reason it stopped. Clear prospects deletes what that run created so the campaign can run again without dedupe blocking the same brands. Use it for test runs or a run with bad keywords.

How the score works

  • A. Need for video volume (40): active Meta ads, video share, new ads in 30 days, other paid channels, number of products.
  • B. Ability to pay (25): headcount (11 to 50 is the sweet spot), traffic proxy (visits, Amazon reviews, Shopify Plus), growth signals, pays for creators.
  • C. Opportunity fit (20): creative gap, where their video comes from (UGC creators is the best fit), a trigger event.
  • D. Accessibility (15): decision maker active on LinkedIn, a second contact with email, no gatekeeper.

Every signal and its points are listed on the prospect page. The weights can be changed in Settings.

The prospect page

Left: the score breakdown, the sample ads, Tracking (connection, stage, outcome, notes) and the event trail. Right: the suggested product with photo check and gallery, the Video URL box, the three messages with Copy and Send by email, the follow-ups, and every fact the run found, all editable. Fix a wrong fact, click Save and recalculate, and the score and messages update. Buttons at the top open the decision maker's LinkedIn profile and the brand Instagram.

The four hand-checked signals

A live run reads everything it can, but four signals still need a minute of your time, exactly as in the tracker. Without them most brands land at 40 to 55 (Later and Possible). With them, the best move into Strong and Must target.
  • DM active 90d (8 points for Y): open their LinkedIn profile, have they posted or engaged in the last 90 days?
  • Other paid channels (up to 5): Google, TikTok, YouTube or Amazon ads besides Meta? Count them, 0 to 3.
  • Pays for creative (5): do their ads show paid creators or UGC? The run sets Y when it sees #ad or "with", check the rest.
  • Trigger event (6): the run sets it when they are hiring a marketing role or launched 10+ ads this month. Add any other (launch, funding).

Working method: open the top brands by score, fill those four, Save and recalculate. Messages stay as they are.

Outreach

By hand on LinkedIn (no provider connected)

  1. Open the prospect, click Copy on the connection note, paste it into LinkedIn's connection request.
  2. In Tracking set LinkedIn connection to Applied. The stage becomes Request sent.
  3. When they accept, set it to Connected. The stage becomes Accepted and the brand appears on Ready to send.
  4. Make the video, paste the link into Video URL, Save. Message A now carries the link.
  5. On Ready to send click Copy, paste into LinkedIn, then Mark Msg 1 sent. Follow-up drafts are scheduled for 3 and 7 days later.
  6. When they reply, set the stage to Replied (or Dead). Follow-ups cancel themselves.

By email

If the prospect has an email and a sender address is set, Send by email on Message A or B sends it from your address, records the send and the first open, sets the stage to Msg 1 and schedules the follow-up drafts. Message A needs the video link first.

Automatic LinkedIn

Set VO_LINKEDIN_PROVIDER to unipile with its three keys (or dryrun to rehearse). Then, per campaign, tick Auto-send connection requests and choose up to which priority number. Every 10 minutes the worker then:

  • Sends one connection request at a time, a random 10 to 30 minutes apart, weekdays 8am to 6pm US Eastern, highest priority first, never above the daily cap (20, hard limit 25) or the weekly cap (100).
  • Checks for acceptances.
  • Marks a request Pending after 7 days and withdraws it after 21 days (the brand goes Dead unless you also used email or Instagram).
  • Reads new LinkedIn messages from your prospects.

After a connection is accepted

  1. The prospect becomes Connected, the stage Accepted, and the draft of Message A is refreshed with the latest observation.
  2. It appears on Ready to send with the draft and a box for the video link.
  3. You make the video, paste the link, check the words, click Send on LinkedIn. The message goes through the provider, the stage becomes Msg 1, and follow-up drafts are scheduled.

Follow-ups

Follow-up 1 is due 3 days after Message 1, Follow-up 2 after 7 days. They appear under Due follow-ups on the Ready to send tab with Send now and Skip. If the campaign has auto follow-ups on, the worker sends them on the day. A reply, Call booked, Pilot, Client or Dead cancels any that are still due.

Replies

A reply on LinkedIn (with a provider) sets the stage to Replied, stores the text on the prospect, cancels follow-ups and emails you the message. The reply is read as Positive (they want it), Negative (no thanks), Question or Neutral: the email subject says which, the prospect shows a coloured chip, and a one-line summary says what they want. Nothing is sent back. Without a provider, paste the reply into Record a reply on the prospect page and the same happens.

Safety limits

Settings, LinkedIn caps: requests per day (hard limit 25), messages per day, requests per week, the priority cut-off and the send-window time zone. If LinkedIn or the provider reports a restriction or a checkpoint, everything pauses, a red banner appears at the top of Video Outreach, and you get an email. Resume from Settings once you have checked the account.

Results and tuning

Reading Results

Reply, call and pilot rates by priority band and by message variant, against contacted prospects. If Possible replies as often as Strong, the 65 boundary is too strict. If B out-replies A, lead with permission.

Changing the weights

Settings, Score weights. Bands read "from this value upwards scores this many points", written threshold:points. Preview impact shows how many of the 74 tracker brands would change band. Save and re-score all applies the new weights to every prospect. Reset to v1 puts the original back.

Settings

What is on the screen

  • Providers: each key set or not set, the AI model, the Apify actor, the LinkedIn provider with a Test connection button, and the plan gate.
  • Default service profile and Global exclusion list (household names, one per line).
  • LinkedIn caps and state (Running or Paused with the reason, and Resume).
  • Score weights and Industry presets (keyword sets saved from a campaign).
